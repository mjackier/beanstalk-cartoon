Privacy Policy

Beanstalk Storybook · last updated November 1, 2026

Beanstalk Storybook is a student project (MCON 368, Touro University) that turns a photo into a cartoon "Jack and the Beanstalk" picture book. This page explains what it collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to show who is signed in and to keep your books in your own account.
  • The cartoon images and book details you create: the character image, the book pages, and the "About Me" details you type (name, age, favorite things, a short description). These are saved so your book is still there when you come back.
  • An activity log: when you sign in, create a book, generate an image or download a PDF, with the time. This helps us debug the app.

What we don't keep

  • Your uploaded photo is not stored. It is sent once to OpenAI's image service to draw the cartoon character and then discarded. Only the cartoon is saved.
  • We don't sell, share or advertise with your information.

Services we use

  • Google for sign-in.
  • OpenAI to generate the images (your photo and the page prompts are sent to their API).
  • Render to host the app and its database.

Your choices

You can delete any of your books from the "My books" page at any time; deleting a book deletes its images. To have your whole account removed, email mjackier@student.touro.edu.

Beanstalk Storybook

Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Beanstalk Storybook is a student project (MCON 368, Touro University) that turns one photo into a 20-page picture book. This page explains what it keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address and profile picture, to show who is signed in and keep your books in your account.
  • Your books: the cartoon portrait made from your photo, the 20 page pictures, the details you type for the About Me page, and the finished PDF, so you can download it again later.
  • Payments: the amounts you add and spend, and the last four digits of the card. Card details go to Stripe, never to us.
  • An activity log: sign-ins, books made, downloads and payments, with the time, to keep the app working.

What we don't keep

  • Your photo is never stored. It is sent to OpenAI's image service to draw the cartoon portrait and then discarded. Only the cartoon is kept.
  • We don't sell, share or advertise with your information.

Services we use

  • Google for sign-in.
  • OpenAI to draw and check the pictures.
  • Stripe for payments.
  • Render to host the app and its database.

Your choices

You can delete any book from My books at any time; that deletes its pictures. To have your whole account removed, email mjackier@student.touro.edu.
